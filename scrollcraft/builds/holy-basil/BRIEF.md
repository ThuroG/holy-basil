# Brief: Holy Basil Landing Page

*Authored under user direction & project brand specifications.*

---

## 1. The Eight Topics

### 1. Vibe in 3–5 words & references
* **Vibe:** Bangkok Wok, 90s Boom-Bap, Street Graffiti, Züri Urban.
* **References:**
  1. *Bangkok Yaowarat Night Market*: Intense wok flames, sizzling holy basil leaves, red bird's eye chilis, sensory street rush.
  2. *90s NYC / Zurich Golden Era Hip-Hop*: Bold black ink outlines, sticker tags on streetlight poles, raw cassette/boombox typography.
  3. *Zurich Kreis Cheib / Langstrasse*: Unpretentious, late-night, multi-cultural street food culture, local neighborhood pride ("Rep your Hood").

### 2. The Scroll Journey (Act by Act)
* **Act 1 – The Street Drop (Hero):** Dimensional layered brand emblem hovering over dark asphalt textures with street-art halo glow and sizzling wok atmosphere.
* **Act 2 – The Zurich Streetfood Dilemma vs. Pad Krapao Revolution:** Sharp contrast between 25-CHF overpriced mediocrity and Holy Basil's radical One-Dish Mastery (100% vegan sauce base, CHF 12–15).
* **Act 3 – The Sizzle (The Emotional Peak):** Full-bleed sensory showcase of the 3 variants (Holy Vegan, Holy Chicken, Holy Pork/Mix) plus crispy Kai Dao egg and Thai Iced Tea.
* **Act 4 – Where's the Truck? (Live Tour & Züri-Hood Tracker):** Interactive Tour-Kalender & neighborhood finder ("Track the Truck") with instant Newsletter alert subscription.
* **Act 5 – The Gateway (Order & Pickup):** Direct commitment: Launch the Mobile QR-Order App (`/order`) with Züri-Code pickup system preview.

### 3. The Energy Curve
* **Act 1:** Electric & Confident (Bold opening punch).
* **Act 2:** Focused & Direct (Crisp typography, high readability, philosophical clarity).
* **Act 3 (Peak):** Maximum Sizzle & Heat (Visceral wok intensity, macro textures, high excitement).
* **Act 4:** Curious & Communal (Quartier pride, local dates, anticipatory calm).
* **Act 5:** High-Voltage Call to Action (Immediate utility: Order now).

### 4. The Feeling Curve & The Peak
* **Act 1:** *Intrigue* – An unconventional streetfood brand that breaks all Thai restaurant clichés.
* **Act 2:** *Relief & Alignment* – "Finally, authentic streetfood without 50 menu items and fair Swiss pricing."
* **Act 3 (PEAK):** *Visceral Craving & Hunger* – Watching the fiery wok toss holy basil leaves, crispy edges of the Kai Dao egg, and vibrant sauces.
* **Act 4:** *Anticipation* – "I need to know when this truck is parking in my Kreis."
* **Act 5:** *Empowerment* – One tap to order with an authentic Züri-Code.

* **The Peak Moment:** The sensory Wok Sizzle in Act 3 where holy basil leaves burst in the pan over smoking duftreis.
* **The Tell-Someone Sentence:** *"It's the site where authentic Bangkok Pad Krapao sizzles with 90s hip-hop energy, and you can track the foodtruck directly to your Zürcher Quartier."*

### 5. Signature Move
* **"The Züri-Code Hood Dial / Split-Flap Scrubber"**:
  An interactive, physical-feeling urban dial/split-flap mechanism that lets the visitor scrub through Zurich postal codes (`8004 LANGSTRASSE`, `8005 ZÜRI WEST`, `8050 OERLIKON`, `8400 WINTI`), dynamically flipping the truck arrival dates, neighborhood stats, and local crew tags in real time!

### 6. Aesthetic Range
* **Urban Street / Graffiti & Modern Dark Grotesque**:
  Deep pitch black canvas (`#0A0A0A`), layered neon-herb greens (`#1B4D2E`, `#2E7D32`, `#52C45A`), street-gold accents (`#FFD54F`), crisp chalk-white copy (`#F5F7F5`). No sterile corporate minimalism, no generic AI claymation.

### 7. World Structure
* **Distinct Scenes with Purposeful Motion**:
  5 deliberate acts with dimensional layering, parallax depth in the hero, kinetic typography, and interactive split-flap elements.
